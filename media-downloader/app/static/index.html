Media Grabber

Paste a link to save a video as MP4/MP3, or a PDF/PPT as a single PDF.

Video → MP4 / MP3

YouTube (incl. Shorts) and most video sites. Add a start/end time to download just that part (seconds, MM:SS or HH:MM:SS). Leave both empty for the whole video.

PDF / PPT → PDF

Any link that shows a PDF or presentation: Google Drive, Google Slides/Docs, Dropbox, online viewers, or a direct .pdf / .ppt / .pptx file. The viewer is scrolled to the end and every page is saved at full resolution.


Only download content you own or have permission to save.
